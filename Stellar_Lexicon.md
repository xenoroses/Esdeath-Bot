### **Command: ,synapse essence**
- **The Essence:** The definitive visualization of a user's unified social identity. It extracts raw spectral data from the server's relational database and projects it into a high-density data card. This is the "Heart" of Hyacine, displaying global standing, historical achievements, and current psychological state at a glance. It represents the ultimate "Social Passport."
- **Technical Matrix:** `[user]` (Optional): A Mention, User ID, or Name. Defaults to the executor. Output: Rich Embed (Hyacine Lavender).
- **Usage Example:** `,synapse essence @Phantom`
- **Operational Scenarios:**
    - *Scenario A (Self-Audit):* After a month of heavy activity, you want to see your growth. You type `,synapse essence`. You see your score at 9.2/10 and your "Aeon Emissary" status.
    - *Scenario B (Security Scan):* You see a suspicious account. You type `,synapse essence @Suspect`. The engine reveals a 1.2 Trust Score and 3 active "Fixation Traces," marking them as a threat.
    - *Scenario C (Legacy Check):* You want to see the accomplishments of a veteran. You type `,synapse essence @Veteran`. Their "Pantheon" status and high "Renown" are displayed in a premium lavender frame.

- **The Essence:** Proposes a permanent binary social link between two identities. This bond is etched into the profile and is visible to the entire sector during scans. It represents the highest tier of voluntary social allegiance between two individuals, creating a shared fate within the platform's social graph.
- **Technical Matrix:** `[user]` (Required): The entity to propose a bond with. Must be different from the executor.
- **Usage Example:** `,synapse resonance @Soulmate`
- **Operational Scenarios:**
    - *Scenario A (The Pact):* Two users decide to lock their fates together. User A types `,synapse resonance @UserB`. User B receives a pulsed notification and must accept to seal the deal.
    - *Scenario B (Public Alliance):* A sector leader wants to publicly acknowledge their top lieutenant. By using resonance, they create a visible link that others can see, signaling a unified front.
    - *Scenario C (Dissolution):* If the bond source fractures, running the command again on the partner allows for the "Resonance severance," removing the link from both profiles.
